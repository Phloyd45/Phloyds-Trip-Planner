Fuel cost estimate
Calculate a route first — this fills in with fuel prices detected for each country you'll cross.
Prices are rough recent averages by country, not a live feed — edit any row to match prices you know are current. Electric charging cost is a single rate since roadside prices vary far more than fuel does. Fill-up stops search OpenStreetMap for up to 3 fuel or charging options within 20 miles of each calculated point along your route and appear as rows in the trip table above — always double-check a station is open/compatible before relying on it.
Toll cost estimate
Calculate a route first — this fills in with a rough toll estimate for each country you'll cross.
Rough averages, not a live tolling feed — motorway tolls are estimated per km driven in that country, vignette countries (e.g. Switzerland, Austria) show a flat trip charge, and countries that are mostly toll-free default to £0. Edit any row to match a route you know, or zero one out if you're avoiding toll roads — this tool doesn't know which specific roads your route uses, only which countries it passes through.

The loop is found by testing rings of stops around your start and growing or shrinking the ring until the total driving time lands near your target — so treat the stops as a suggested shape for the trip rather than destinations chosen for their own sake. "Open in Road Trip" hands the same loop over to the Road Trip tab, where you get fuel and toll estimates, overnight stops, exports and printing.
